# Focus Realm brand rules

- **Logo:** FR monogram (deep-blue F `#0b3a91` interlocked with light-blue R `#5aa8dc→#8ccbe9`) in a white square tile. Stacked "Focus / Realm" wordmark in bold black. Monogram alone in small placements. Source: `components/site/Logo.tsx`.
- **Colour:** primary blue `#2a5bd7` (CTA pills, accents, charts), hover `#1e47b8`; headlines `#0f1222`; body `#3d4256`; secondary text `#4f5568`; page `#f4f5fb` (lavender-grey); cards/tiles `#ffffff`; yellow `#f5c542` rare accent only.
- **Type:** Poppins. Headlines bold/extra-bold, tight tracking, black; body regular, dark grey.
- **Feel:** clean, flat, minimal ("Strategic Minimalism"), with purposeful motion: orbiting hero, marquee, animated borders and gradients. All loops stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
